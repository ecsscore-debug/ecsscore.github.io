Our Mission

Create a verified, portable & trusted
career identity for every professional

Our mission is to make employment credibility universal, portable, and proof-driven — so that talent is judged by verified truth instead of claims, bias or incomplete information.

We are building a standardized, tamper-proof employment identity layer — the ECS Score — to bring truth, transparency and trust into India’s hiring ecosystem.

The Problem We Solve

  • • Fake experience and unverifiable roles are rising rapidly
  • • References are inconsistent and often unreliable
  • • Screenshots and self-claims are misused at scale
  • • Companies lose time and money due to poor hiring accuracy
  • • Deserving candidates get filtered out because of broken trust

How We Deliver

We take a verification-first approach. Every signal that contributes to the ECS Score is grounded in proof — employer confirmation, peer validation, and document authenticity.

The result is a fairer system where careers are built on accuracy, trust and credibility.
